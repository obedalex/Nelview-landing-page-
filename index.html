What exactly is Nelview?
An AI peer-review and research-evaluation platform. Upload a manuscript, grant, proposal, thesis or abstract and get a structured, publication-level review with a 0–100 readiness score across methodology, novelty, statistics and ethics, plus prioritised recommendations.
How is it different from Grammarly or ChatGPT?
Those tools focus on grammar and generating content. Nelview doesn't write your paper. It evaluates it the way a reviewer, supervisor or editor would, against the standards that decide whether work gets published.
How is Nelview different from Google's Paper Assistant Tool (PAT)?
PAT is a Google Research tool built to catch mathematical and logical errors in computer science papers. So far it has been offered free to authors through pilots at specific conferences such as ICML and STOC. It deliberately doesn't score or rank papers. Nelview is built for every discipline and every stage of research: theses, grant proposals, ethics applications and journal manuscripts, not only CS conference papers. You get a 0–100 readiness verdict with prioritised next steps, journal-fit and predatory-journal checks, the option to add a named human expert, and pricing and supervisor workflows designed for African universities, in English and French. The two tools share a view we agree with: AI should help authors catch problems before submission, not replace peer review.
What document types can it review?
Journal manuscripts, grant proposals, research proposals, theses and dissertations, ethics applications, conference abstracts and book proposals. The type is detected automatically.
Can I get a real human review too?
Yes. On any verdict you can commission named experts matched by field and h-index: one reviewer, two, or a full editorial panel. Double-blind, with a signed report in 5–14 days.
Does it support African journals and contexts?
It's core to our mission. The formatter and Journal Check cover African and international venues, and legitimacy is verified against DOAJ and COPE.
Is my work safe and private?
Your document is processed only to produce your review. It is never used to train models and never sold. You keep full ownership of your work.
