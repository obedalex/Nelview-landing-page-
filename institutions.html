Questions from universities

Something else? Book a call.

How is institutional pricing calculated?
Per licensed seat per academic year, with volume tiers. Supervisors and admins are free. Pilots for a single programme are available for one semester.
Do students ever pay?
No. Students on a licensed seat get unlimited AI reviews under your criteria and never see a paywall.
Which systems do you integrate with?
SAML and Google SSO, plus Moodle and Canvas. Student rosters can be imported by CSV or synced from your LMS.
Where is our data stored?
Documents are processed only to produce reviews, never used for training, and never sold. Data residency options are available for institutions that need them.
What happens when students graduate?
With supervisor approval, graduates can move their projects to a personal Nelview account and keep their review history. Their seat returns to your pool.
