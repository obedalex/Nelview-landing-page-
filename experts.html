Reviewer questions

Who can apply?
Professors, postdocs and research consultants with a verifiable publication record and peer-review experience in their field.
How and when am I paid?
Payment is held in escrow when you're awarded a brief and released to your payout account once the author accepts your report.
What does verification involve?
ORCID and affiliation checks, a publication review and a short identity (KYC) step required for payouts.
Can I review for institutions too?
Yes. Partner universities can invite approved experts as external reviewers for theses and high-stakes submissions.
