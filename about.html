Good research shouldn't wait months to be judged.

Nelview began with a question researchers across Africa kept asking and rarely got answered in time: is this research strong, and what needs to improve?

Try NelviewMeet the team
01Why Nelview exists

The talent is here. The feedback isn't.

Across the continent, students and early-career researchers are producing important work. But the systems that decide whether that work is ready (supervisors, reviewers, editors) are stretched thin, and most of them are owned elsewhere.

So researchers move between AI tools, literature tools, busy supervisors and slow journal systems. Feedback arrives late, if at all, often only once a draft is already due or a manuscript has already been rejected.

19%of manuscripts received a first decision within one month
10%waited more than six months for one
Source: study of 2,520 manuscripts

Brilliant work shouldn't stall for lack of review, mentorship or funding.

02

So we built the missing layer.

A rigorous, affordable review before you submit, shaped by the professors who set these standards. It judges your work. It never writes it.

Research

Is the question sound?

Methodology, structure and argument, judged the way your document type is judged in the real world.

Evidence

Does the evidence hold?

Citations checked against real sources, statistics tested for integrity, and gaps flagged before a reviewer finds them.

Integrity

Is it ready, and honest?

Responsible-AI use, predatory-journal risk and research ethics, all in one readiness verdict you can act on.

03

Feedback is the start, not the finish line.

A stronger paper can lead to another discovery, a better policy, a new product. So we treat what happens after review as part of the work.

  • Pairing researchers with mentors and named experts in their field.
  • Opening pathways to journals, grants and funders.
  • Returning a share of every subscription to early-stage African research through the Research Pledge.
Our mission

To give every researcher supervisor-level feedback before they submit, and to keep the value that creates on the continent.

Our vision

Research infrastructure Africa owns, where African researchers produce the evidence that shapes the continent's future.

What we believe

01

Evaluate, never ghost-write.

Nelview judges your work. It doesn't write it, so what you submit stays yours and stands up to any integrity check.

02

Rigour should be affordable.

Supervisor-level feedback shouldn't depend on which university you attend or how much time your supervisor has.

03

You own your work.

Documents are never stored beyond review, never used to train models and never sold. Read our privacy policy.
